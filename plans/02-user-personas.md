## Persona 1: "The Overwhelmed Beginner" — Sarah, 32

First-time homeowner. Watched YouTube videos about growing tomatoes. Excited but paralyzed by complexity: when to start seeds, how deep, how far apart, when to transplant...

**Current behavior:** 14 browser tabs open. Started a spreadsheet, abandoned it. Bought seeds impulsively with no plan.

**Pain points:** Information overload, unknown unknowns, existing apps assume baseline knowledge, no feedback loop.

**What Tend gives her:** She says "I just bought a house in Portland, OR, I have a 10x12 raised bed that gets afternoon sun, and I want to grow vegetables for my family of three." The agent builds a complete plan, tells her exactly what to buy and when, then proactively manages her schedule with daily "here's what to do" messages.

**Willingness to pay:** $5-8/month — "a knowledgeable gardening friend who never gets tired of your questions."

---

## Persona 2: "The Drowning Enthusiast" — Marcus, 48

15 years of experience. 6 raised beds, a greenhouse, 40+ varieties. Knows what he's doing but drowning in logistics.

**Current behavior:** Paper journal + Google Sheets + GrowVeg + weather apps + memory. Loses track of crop rotation. Misses optimal harvest windows.

**What Tend gives him:** Automatic crop rotation planning from history, generated seed starting calendars, frost risk alerts 48 hours out, real-time schedule management. "What should I grow in bed 3 this year?" considers 3-year rotation history.

**Willingness to pay:** $8-15/month — replaces GrowVeg ($29/yr) and consolidates 4-5 tools.

---

## Persona 3: "The Community Garden Coordinator" — Diana, 55

Manages a 30-plot community garden. Coordinates volunteers, tracks plot assignments, manages shared resources.

**What Tend gives her:** Multi-user workspace where each gardener gets agent-managed plots. Automated member onboarding, volunteer scheduling, aggregate reporting.

**Willingness to pay:** $20-40/month organizational tier.

---

## Persona 4: "The Micro-Farm Operators" — Jake & Ava, 35 and 33

1-acre market garden, 60+ varieties with succession planting, selling at farmers markets and to restaurants.

**What Tend gives them:** Succession planting calendar management, daily harvest task lists, yield tracking via quick messages ("harvested 12 lbs lettuce mix from bed A3"), seed order planning from actual data.

**Willingness to pay:** $15-25/month — replaces multiple tools, saves hours/week.

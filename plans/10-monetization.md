## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Target | Features |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Seedling** (Free) | $0 | Onboarding / trial | 1 garden, 10 plants, 20 messages/day, no proactive alerts |
| **Grower** | $7.99/mo or $59.99/yr | Home gardeners | Unlimited gardens/plants/chat, proactive weather alerts, photo health assessment, crop rotation, season history |
| **Cultivator** | $14.99/mo or $119.99/yr | Serious growers / micro-farms | Succession planting automation, harvest tracking & analytics, seed inventory, multi-season predictions, data export |
| **Steward** | $29.99/mo | Community gardens | Multi-user workspace (50 plots), coordinator dashboard, member onboarding, volunteer scheduling, aggregate reporting |

---

## Additional Revenue Streams

- **Seed/supply affiliate partnerships** — agent recommends specific seeds -> affiliate link to Johnny's Seeds, Baker Creek, etc.
- **Sensor integration partnerships** — premium integration with soil moisture sensors, weather stations (Tempest, Ecowitt)
- **Anonymized cross-garden intelligence data** — long-term, with consent. What varieties perform best in which regions.
- **Seasonal content/workshops** — paid masterclasses or guided seasonal programs run through the agent.

---

## Conversion Expectations

Industry benchmarks show mobile app trial-to-paid conversion of 3-10%. Planta's data shows users tracking 5+ plants almost universally upgrade within 3 weeks. Tend's agent-first approach should drive higher engagement (daily touchpoints via proactive messages), pushing conversion toward the upper end.

**Target:** 8-12% trial-to-paid conversion, 70%+ annual retention for paying users.
